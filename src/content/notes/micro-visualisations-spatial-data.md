making the claim. Read the dashed circle, not the outline. At resolution 7 the target is a speck
inside the one cell you were given. By 11 it nearly fills a footprint that has
stopped being a hexagon, which is what 217 cells bought.

Each is under a kilobyte, needs no charting library, and has fixed `width` and
`height`, so it cannot shift the layout. `currentColor` serves both themes from
one file. Best of all, the script that computes the table emits the glyphs, so
the picture cannot drift from the numbers beside it.

A figure does not have to be a page wide to be a figure.
